# Brief: "ShopEase" mobile store

We sell clothing and accessories and want our own mobile shopping app for Android and iOS.

Customers should be able to:
- create an account and sign in
- browse products by category and search them
- see product details with photos, sizes and colors
- add products to a cart and change quantities
- check out and pay by card
- see their past orders and each order's status

We want a first version live quickly, then grow it. Keep it simple, fast and easy to use.
